One terminal, shared by you and your agents.

Any program in tmux, on all your machines, behind one hub you run. Open it from a browser, your phone or the desktop app. Agents drive the same terminals from the CLI.

$ curl -fsSL https://offdesk.dev/install | sh
0:agent1:app2:nas
$ T=$(offdesk open nas --cwd ~/app --cmd claude --json | jq -r .id)
$ offdesk send $T "fix the failing tests"
$ offdesk wait $T --silence 5000 --timeout 600
quiet for 5s
$ offdesk read $T --lines 80
  ⏺ Ran pnpm test: 214 passed
  ⏺ Fixed the date parsing in src/report.ts
$
[offdesk]0:agent*opened by agentnas · ~/app
